Privacy Policy Effective Date: 30 July 2026 Thank you for using Triad Trainer. Your privacy is important. This Privacy Policy explains what information Triad Trainer collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data. Information We Collect Triad Trainer is designed to help users learn and practice guitar triads. The app stores practice-related information on your device, including: - Lessons completed - Practice progress - Practice statistics - App settings This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us. Personal Information Triad Trainer does not collect, store, or share personally identifiable information such as: - Name - Email address - Phone number - Physical address - Payment information Analytics Triad Trainer does not use analytics services to track your activity. Advertising Triad Trainer does not display third-party advertisements. Data Sharing We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Data Storage All practice data and settings are stored locally on your device. If you uninstall the app, this data may be deleted unless your device backs it up. Children's Privacy Triad Trainer is suitable for users of all ages. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Security Because your practice data is stored locally on your device, keeping your device secure (such as using a screen lock) helps protect your information.
